Why Graphical Passwords?

Images are easier for humans to remember — and harder for computers to brute-force.

🔒

PBKDF2-SHA256

Your click sequence is never stored. It is hashed with 210,000 iterations and a unique cryptographic salt — just like top-tier text passwords.

🗺️

Order Matters

The system remembers which points you clicked and in what order. Same spots in a different order means access denied.

📐

Screen-Size Proof

Points are mapped to a logical 10×10 grid, so your password works regardless of screen size or browser zoom level.

⏱️

Rate Limited Login

After too many failed attempts, the login endpoint is temporarily locked. Brute-forcing graphical passwords becomes computationally infeasible.

🍪

Secure Sessions

Sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite:Strict cookies stored in a SQLite-backed session store — not vulnerable in-memory storage.

🛡️

Security Headers

Helmet.js enforces Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, and other hardening headers on every response.

Built With

Node.js · Express.js · SQLite · PBKDF2-SHA256 · Vanilla JS · Helmet
